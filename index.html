Health Professions Education & Research Department

HPERD

The Health Professions Education & Research Department (HPERD) at Faisalabad Medical University (FMU) is dedicated to strengthening health professions education, promoting faculty development, and advancing research in medical and health sciences education.

Through faculty development, educational innovation, research, assessment, mentorship, and continuing professional development, HPERD supports educators and learners in building a culture of excellence, scholarship, and lifelong learning across FMU.

About the Department

Building better educators, researchers and healthcare professionals.

HPERD provides an academic platform for professional development, educational research, curriculum enhancement, assessment, mentorship, and innovation within health professions education.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

HPERD is committed to creating an academic environment where educators and learners can continuously improve, innovate, collaborate, and contribute to better health professions education.

Our Mission

Mission

To strengthen the quality of health professions education at Faisalabad Medical University by developing educators, promoting evidence-based teaching and assessment practices, supporting educational research, and fostering innovation and collaboration across the health sciences.

Our Vision

Vision

To be a leading centre for health professions education and educational research, recognized for excellence in faculty development, innovative teaching, meaningful scholarship, and continuous improvement in healthcare education.

Leadership

Message from the Vice Chancellor

Vice Chancellor's Message

"Faisalabad Medical University is committed to nurturing an academic culture in which excellence in teaching, learning, research, and professional development remains a shared responsibility. The Health Professions Education & Research Department provides an important platform for strengthening this culture across the University."

Through HPERD, our faculty have opportunities to enhance their teaching skills, engage in educational scholarship, and adopt contemporary approaches to curriculum development, assessment, and learner support.

Prof. Dr. Tariq Mehmood

Vice Chancellor, Faisalabad Medical University

Department Leadership

Message from the In-Charge, HPERD

In-Charge's Message

"At HPERD, we believe that effective health professions education is built through continuous learning, thoughtful reflection, collaboration, and evidence-informed practice."

Our aim is to provide faculty and learners with meaningful opportunities to develop their educational skills, explore new approaches to teaching and assessment, and contribute to research that addresses real challenges in health professions education. We look forward to working with colleagues across Faisalabad Medical University to create a stronger and more innovative educational environment.

Dr. Ayesha Malik

In-Charge, Health Professions Education & Research Department
