Effective Feedback in Clinical Teaching

14 October 2026, 9:00 AM HPERD Seminar Hall, FMU Main Campus

This half-day, hands-on workshop equips clinical supervisors with structured, learner-centred techniques for giving feedback during ward rounds, bedside teaching, and OSCE stations. Participants will practise the feedback models covered through short peer role-plays.
