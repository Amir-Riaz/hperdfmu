About HPERD

Building the teaching, assessment, and research capacity of Faisalabad Medical University's health professions faculty.

Department Overview

Who we are

The Health Professions Education & Research Department (HPERD) was established at Faisalabad Medical University to strengthen how the university teaches, assesses, and studies the practice of medical education. HPERD works across FMU's constituent colleges — supporting faculty with structured training, applied assessment reform, and mentored research.

Our work spans three areas: faculty development programs that build teaching skill; curriculum and assessment consultation for academic departments; and a research unit that studies education practice and publishes findings for the wider medical education community in Pakistan.

Vision

To be a recognised centre of excellence in health professions education and research in Pakistan, shaping how future clinicians are trained and assessed.

Mission

To equip FMU faculty with modern teaching and assessment methods, and to generate rigorous, locally relevant research that improves medical education practice.

Objectives

  • Deliver structured faculty development programs
  • Support evidence-based curriculum reform
  • Build a sustainable culture of education research
  • Strengthen assessment reliability and validity

Message from the Department

"HPERD exists to make good teaching a deliberate, learnable practice — not an accident of individual talent. Every program we run is built to give FMU faculty tools they can use in their next lecture, ward round, or exam."
